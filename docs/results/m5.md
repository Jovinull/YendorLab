## 2. Audit of the M4 benchmark

The comparison was not equivalent, in three ways: unequal exposure through the no-progress limit, a reference policy that was a fixed direction, and a stair-using heuristic counted across depths.

Replay of the M4 final seeds with per-depth accounting (EXP-0023, `d52faf66-aab7-4521-8c20-b69a01fd1e00`) reproduced every M4 mean exactly:

| Agent | Mean new cells | Depth 1 | Other depths | Mean turns | Past depth 1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Initial linear | 78.0 | 78.0 | 0 | 12.5 | 0/16 |
| Trained A | 194.6 | 194.6 | 0 | 21.4 | 0/16 |
| Trained B | 229.1 | 229.1 | 0 | 22.3 | 0/16 |
| Random | 160.1 | 160.1 | 0 | 92.0 | 0/16 |
| Heuristic | 502.0 | 455.1 | 46.9 | 65.8 | 2/16 |

Stairs gave the heuristic 9% of its total. H3 is supported: the eight-move planner beats the original on depth 1 at equal budget (985.2 against 649.1 on final seeds, not paired, because the actions differ). The heuristic's lead over M4 came from planning. Its own weakness is survival: it never fights back, and 47 of 100 runs died.

Under the fair task the M4 learning claim does not hold. On 100 final seeds, M4-A against random is +17.3 cells, 95% interval [−16.3, 53.2]; against fixed north +70.2 [43.9, 101.1]; on the relay against random −8.4 [−45.8, 29.8]. M4 runs remain unchanged as historical records, but they measured a change in behavior, not exploration competence above a uniform random mover.

## 3. Source of the northward preference

H1 is supported. Across 64 diagnostic seeds, depth 1 starts at seven positions, 53 of them at (38,26), with 26 rows of map to the north and 2 to the south. Depth 2 arrivals have 58 distinct positions among 60 seeds. So one action-indexed weight can exploit the map frame with no spatial skill: fixed north is the best fixed direction on depth 1 (187.2 cells) and collapses on the relay (31.2). M4-A pressed `k` 40% of the time and `y`, `l`, `j` almost never.

## 4. Environment certification

**Perception.** Privileged test build, with truth on stderr only (`scripts/test-native-perception.py`): 446 observations compared to ground truth; 3,528 unseen monster and 3,881 unseen item instances never appeared; 150 secret cells matched their appearance; magic mapping, telepathy, darkness, detect magic and hallucination flowed through the real item UI; 246 hallucinated item observations exposed no real name. Differential test: on 80 world pairs differing only in hidden truth (unseen item identity, unseen creature HP, secret doors and hidden traps, including depth 8), the agent stream was byte-identical. The production binary contains none of this code, asserted by grep. These cover the listed cases, not every interaction.

**Protocol.** 14 transport tests with a scripted fake engine: split lines, two lines per read, lines above the pipe size, stderr floods, invalid UTF-8, garbage, silent exit, hangs at reset and step, broken sequence, seed mismatch. 3,000 consecutive real resets: zero failures, no leftover directories, 55 resets per second. Long random episodes reached up to 4,114 events before natural death, sampled child RSS 5.4–5.7 MiB, with no growth trend. No random walker survived to the 5,000-event cap.

**Rewards.** Reset grants nothing. Rest and revisits grant nothing. `cells_v2` pays 0.1 per new start-floor cell and −1 on death. Budget ends bootstrap; depth exits and natural ends do not. A failed episode produces no update. No double counting found; the M4 `exploration_v1` depth reward made descending valuable by construction, because a new level's first view counted as discovery, which is why the v2 floor task ends at a depth change. The primary metric reads the observation stream, not reward.

**Reproducibility.** `trace_sha256` over every native line and action. Replay is exact with fixed policy RNG on the same build. M4-A reproduced 194.5625 cells and 1,401 events on the hardened stack. Crash resume reproduces the weights of an uninterrupted run (section 9).

Also passing on the final patch: ASan and UBSan on real death, 1,600 turnless inputs, and 500 exploration decisions to depth 2. Seed catalog, 6,773 lines equal after declared normalization. Gymnasium `check_env`. Sanitizers did not catch the hang in section 1, which was a logic loop on a specific prompt sequence; passing them says little about such paths.

## 6. Architecture bakeoff

Validation means over 40 seeds, three independent runs. Best checkpoints were chosen on the first 16 of these seeds, so values are optimistic and comparisons are exploratory.

| Method | Per-run | Mean | Compute to policy |
|---|---|---:|---|
| A: TD, `abs_v2` (M4 features) | 355, 305, 333 | 331 | 200 episodes, ~44k decisions |
| A: TD, `ego_patch_v1` | 215, 219, 295 | 243 | 200 episodes, ~43k decisions |
| A: TD, `rel_v3` | 768, 700, 791 | 753 | 200 episodes, ~37k decisions, ~170 s |
| B: BC, `rel_v3` | 965, 958, 971 | 965 | 60 demos, ~15k decisions, 100 CPU s + 6 s fit |
| B: BC, `rel_v3` without planner | 494, 550, 473 | 505 | same, 5 s fit |
| B: BC, `ego_patch_v1` | 446, 450, 442 | 446 | same, 13 s fit |
| C: BC then TD, with planner | 901, 746, 859 | 835 | B + 200 episodes |
| C: BC then TD, without planner | 576, 589, 548 | 571 | B + 200 episodes |
| Cold TD without planner, 400 episodes | 496, 494, 492 | 494 | ~90k decisions |

H4 supported: `rel_v3` more than doubles `abs_v2` under identical training. H5 supported: BC with the planner signal was cheapest and best, and it degraded sharply without that signal. Weights confirm why: a large `on_route` and `enemy` weight simply restates the teacher. On final seeds it is indistinguishable from the planner, +15.4 [−11.8, 40.5]. This is not independent discovery.

H6 rejected for the planner arm. RL from imitation lost skill: 965 fell to 835, and the first validation after 50 episodes fell to 547–670. TD learned to avoid enemies rather than fight, the opposite of the teacher, and dies more (reference TD 9–17 of 40; BC 0). Without the planner, RL added about 66 cells over BC, the only place where RL improved on imitation.

**DAgger.** Accuracy on the learner's own states was about 0.47, far below 0.73 on teacher states, which is real covariate shift. But three iterations did not help: without planner 505 fell to 437, patch 446 to 402. Reasons are not established. Aggregated labels come from a planner whose choice depends on a global map these features cannot see.

**Capacity (EXP-0028b).** Train and held-out accuracy matched at about 0.73, suggesting underfit, so a small MLP was justified. It did not pay off online: no-planner 514, 517, 462 against linear 494, 550, 473; patch 452, 445, 484 against 446, 450, 442. Patch accuracy rose about 5 points. Rejected: more cost and no measured gain. PyTorch, PPO, DQN and evolution were not run; no result here justifies them, and the capacity test argues against model size as the limiting factor.

**Hyperparameters (two runs each).** γ .98 gave the best peak, 826, but latest fell to 705. α .1 was worst, 713. No setting removed the instability below.

**Second stage, 400 episodes, without planner.** Warm start peaked at 100–200 episodes, then declined: best 562, latest 498. Cold start kept rising slowly to about 494. More training did not help on this horizon.

## 7. Ablations

|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|

| A2b | Remove visits, no planner | 235 | 215 | .02 | **Essential** without the planner: the policy loops |

## 8. CPU

Two benchmark runs differ by a factor of about 8 in absolute speed. The first ran at about 10.8 ms per event; the cause was not established. Ratios agree, and the second, on an otherwise idle machine, is the reference.

| Reset, median | s |
|---|---:|
| Mounted binary, mounted cwd, 617 files | 0.477 |
| Linux binary, mounted cwd, 617 files | 0.369 |
| Mounted binary, mounted cwd, empty | 0.047 |
| Mounted binary, tmpfs cwd | 0.025 |
| Linux binary, tmpfs cwd, with or without files | 0.015 |

File accumulation on the mounted directory was the dominant cost, about 10×. Binary location alone is about 1.7×. This corrects the M4 reading.

Per event on a mature map (991 cells): 1.22 ms, of which JSON 0.29, tracking 0.09, native and pipe 0.84. Per decision: perception 0.07 ms, planner 0.19, encoding 0.04, scoring 0.01. TD update 4.6 µs. Checkpoint 1.2 ms on tmpfs. Child RSS 5.7 MiB, campaign Python about 29 MiB.

| Workers | Decisions/s | Episodes/s (budget 150) |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 624 | 4.2 |
| 2 | 1,123 | 7.5 |
| 4 | 1,655 | 11.0 |
| 6 | 2,075 | 13.8 |

Mounted binary, private cwd, one worker: 577 decisions/s. **JSON is a measurable cost but not a blocker.** Sending only changed cells could remove perhaps a third of the per-decision cost, by estimate; it was not built. At these rates a 20,000-episode campaign fits in hours, so a new wire format is not justified now. Learner cost is negligible; the simulation dominates.

Operating mode: Git and sources in the Windows checkout, native binary staged by `scripts/stage-linux.sh` to a path named by its own hash, game directories in tmpfs. `manifest.json` records the hash, and resume refuses any other binary, so the copy cannot diverge unnoticed.

## 9. Reliability

47 campaigns, 11,200 episodes, 2,324,726 decisions on the final binary: zero operational failures. Failure tests with a real engine: crash after journal before checkpoint, crash after checkpoint, controlled stop, SIGTERM, STOP file, larger episode target, changed binary, changed configuration, refusal to overwrite, and an engine that cannot start. Every crash resume produced the same weight hash as an uninterrupted run. Orphan rows are kept, marked superseded. Resume is exact on the same build and platform unless a timing limit fires; that is tested behavior, not a general promise. A detached process survived 75 seconds with no WSL session open; a Windows sleep or reboot is not covered, which is what resume is for. The longest campaign run is 400 episodes, so multi-hour behavior is extrapolated.

Multilevel at 500 decisions: P1 1221.4, P2 683.2, planner seeking stairs 923.0, random 279.4; a floor policy treats stairs as obstacles, so it stays on depth 1 and survives, while the stair seeker dies 53 of 100 times.

**Interpretation.** Direction preference does not explain these gains: the policies carry no direction input, the own-prior control is near random, and the gain survives on depth 2 where position varies. P1 reproduces a planner through one feature. P2 is the honest learned result: a linear policy with local features and episode memory, no route information, beats controls by about 340 cells on unseen seeds on depths 1 and 2 and remains about 400 below the planner. Whether its RL phase adds anything is exploratory evidence from validation only; P2 was not compared with plain imitation on final seeds.

**Limits.** Seeds pair evaluation, so intervals describe map variation for one fixed checkpoint, not variation across training. Three runs cannot estimate that. Depths 1–2, Classic only, same build. Selection used 40 validation seeds, and hyperparameter and second-stage work came after seeing validation data. Commits after the freeze touched campaign recording and tests, not policies, task or evaluation.

## 12. Readiness

**Exploration: `CONDITIONAL_GO`.** Infrastructure is ready, the task is defensible, and the policies are real. But the hypothesis that longer training helps is unsupported: RL degraded the best policy, and the no-planner line peaked within 200 episodes. A long campaign is reasonable only as a bounded investigation of that hypothesis, with the prepared stop rule.

**Navigation: `NO_GO`.** No goal-reaching task exists. The no-planner policy explores; it has not been shown to go somewhere on purpose.

**Full game: `NO_GO`, `FULL_GAME_READY = false`.** Movement only, no item, target or survival contract, no live victory.

## 13. Recommended architecture

Deploy **P1** as Maia's exploration skill: linear `rel_v3` from behavior cloning. It costs about two CPU minutes, needs no further training, and matches the planner. Treat it as a learned wrapper of a hand-written planner, and do not train it further with this RL setup.

Keep training, if anything, **P2's line**: linear TD on `rel_v3` without the planner, warm-started from imitation. It is the only arm where RL added value and where a result would mean something. Direct the next effort at why TD destabilizes before spending hours on it.

## 15. Next work

1. Find why linear TD destabilizes, with a stepped learning rate or averaged weights, on validation only.
2. Define `navigation_v1`: reach known stairs on depth 1–3, with its own controls. This also tests whether memory features support intent.
